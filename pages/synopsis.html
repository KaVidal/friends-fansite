The heartwarming sitcom about six close friends living in New York City, navigating love, careers, and life's ups and downs together with laughter, loyalty, and a lot of coffee at Central Perk.

What is Friends?

Friends is an American sitcom that originally aired on NBC from 1994 to 2004, spanning 10 seasons and 236 episodes. Created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman, the show became one of the most beloved TV series of all time.


The Premise

The series follows the lives of six friends — Rachel Green, Monica Geller, Phoebe Buffay, Joey Tribbiani, Chandler Bing, and Ross Geller — as they navigate relationships, careers, and life in Manhattan. Much of the action takes place in their apartments and at the iconic coffeehouse Central Perk.


Why It's Special

  • Introduced timeless catchphrases like “We were on a break!” and “How you doin'?”.
  • Featured countless guest stars, from Brad Pitt to Bruce Willis.
  • Won Emmys, a Golden Globe, and the hearts of fans worldwide.

Fun Facts

- The show was almost called Insomnia Café before it became Friends.
- The orange couch in Central Perk was found in the Warner Bros. studio basement.
- All six lead actors negotiated equal pay starting in season 3, a rare move at the time.
