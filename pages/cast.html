Behind The Laughs

Meet the six friends and the actors who brought them to life.

Meet the Cast

Main Cast

Rachel Green

Actor: Jennifer Aniston

From runaway bride to fashion icon, Rachel's story blends humor, ambition, and heart.

Monica Geller

Actor: Courteney Cox

A chef with perfectionist energy and endless love for her friends (and clean kitchens).

Phoebe Buffay

Actor: Lisa Kudrow

The quirky free spirit with unforgettable songs and even better one-liners.

Chandler Bing

Actor: Matthew Perry

The king of sarcasm, whose humor hides a heart of gold and a love for Monica.

Ross Geller

Actor: David Schwimmer

A passionate paleontologist whose awkward charm and endless love for Rachel defined the show.

Joey Tribbiani

Actor: Matt LeBlanc

The charming aspiring actor who loves sandwiches almost as much as his friends.

Recurring & Guest Stars

Gunther

Actor: James Michael Tyler

The quiet barista at Central Perk — and Rachel's biggest fan.

Janice

Actor: Maggie Wheeler

“Oh. My. God.” — the unforgettable laugh and presence in Chandler's life.

Mike Hannigan

Actor: Paul Rudd

Phoebe's perfect match: funny, kind, and willing to sing along.
